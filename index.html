Why PeruseLab

Chemical knowledge is valuable. Too often, it is fragmented.

Chemists generate structures, reactions, annotations, observations, and research context across disconnected tools, files, notebooks, presentations, and databases. The result is knowledge that can be hard to find, hard to reuse, and difficult to share across teams.

PeruseLab is designed as a practical chemical informatics workspace: a place where molecular structures and scientific context can be created, searched, documented, visualized, and reused with less friction.

Capabilities

A feature bundle built around real chemistry workflows.

Draw and Search

Sketch molecules and reactions directly in the app, then instantly search for similar structures with relevance scoring.

Chemical Structure Search

Find molecules, reactions, and diagrams by formula or keyword, or search by exact match, substructure, superstructure, and similarity to explore 2D/3D structures.

AI-Assisted Documentation

Powered by Google Gemini, AI helps capture scientific context, generate multilingual documentation, and speed up research workflows.

3D Molecular Visualization

Rich Media Attachments

AI & Multilingual Science

Advanced AI should help chemists, not slow them down.

PeruseLab is evolving toward an AI-enabled scientific platform where chemists can use modern AI capabilities without needing to become AI engineers. The goal is simple: make documentation, translation, knowledge capture, and research communication easier.

Current and planned work includes Google Gemini-powered scientific documentation, multilingual knowledge capture, and future graph neural network models for QSAR and QSPR prediction.
